How to play

Each challenge focuses on a different type of connector. Drag the 3D lego bricks so that the words line up in a grammatical sentence. When every slot is filled, the game will score your sentence automatically. Correct sentences award 10 points.

If you enable the timer, you have 75 seconds to complete the sentence. When the time runs out, you move on to the next round automatically. Use the skip button whenever you want a fresh sentence.

The colours, sizes, and studs help you keep track of each brick while you rotate or drag them into position. Build confidently and explore how discourse markers, linking words, and participle clauses connect ideas.
